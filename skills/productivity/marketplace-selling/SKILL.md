# Second-Hand Marketplace Selling

## Core Philosophy

**You are not selling a used item. You are selling a solution at a discount.**

The #1 reason Marketplace listings fail is **audience mismatch** — the listing attracts the wrong buyer. A workstation laptop attracts people looking for cheap Netflix machines. Fix the audience before fixing the price.

---

## Phase 0: Diagnose Ghosting

Before any tactic, identify the real problem:

| Symptom | Likely Cause |
|---|---|
| "Sigue disponible?" then ghost | Price too high vs. perceived value, or generic listing |
| Ask specs then disappear | Buyer doesn't know WHAT makes this item special |
| Ask + negotiate then vanish | No urgency, no trust, no clear value proposition |
| NO ONE messages | Bad main photo or wrong title keywords |

**Fix the audience first.** A workstation must NOT be described like a generic laptop.

---

## Phase 1: Define the Buyer (3 Profiles)

Segment the market into 3 buyer personas. Each needs a different angle:

| Profile | What They Search | What They Care About | Hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Student** (low budget, needs software) | "laptop para [program]", "workstation barata" | Software included, price, program compatibility | Autodesk/Office bundle |
| **Freelancer/Dev** (knows the hardware) | "ThinkPad usada", "[model] i7", "laptop programación" | RAM expandability, keyboard, ports, Linux | Specs detail, durability |
| **Small office / working pro** (needs reliability) | "laptop oficina profesional usada", "equipo con licencias" | Legal licenses, plug-and-play, durability | "Licencias originales, lista para trabajar" |

**Define who to NOT engage with** — tire-kickers, permuta offers, "envíos" requests. Have a polite but firm filter script ready.

## Phase 2: Photography Strategy

The main photo decides if they open the listing. Invest time here.

### Main Photo Options (pick one):
- **"In Context"** — item in a real work setup (desk, papers, coffee, tools). Shows it's used by a professional.
- **"The Beast"** — side profile emphasizing thickness/ports for rugged items. Contrast with a slim object to show size.
- **"The Detail"** — close-up of the differentiating feature (keyboard, trackpoint, badge, screen). Shows heritage.

### Secondary Photos (mandatory, in order):
1. System info screen showing specs (OS, RAM, processor)
2. License activation proof (Windows/Office activated)
3. All ports/sides visible
4. Model/serial label (snap partially obscured)
5. Accessories included (charger, cables)
6. BIOS/UEFI screen (transparency)
7. Optional: item running a relevant heavy program

### Rules:
- Natural window light (side, not overhead). NO flash.
- Clean background (desk, not bed/sofa). No clutter.
- No hands holding the item. No reflections.
- Same resolution across all photos. Crop, don't stretch.
- Clean screen with microfiber before every shot.

### Video (10-15 seconds):
Marketplace prioritizes video listings. Show: specs screen → open an app → show keyboard/detail → close. Add text overlay with price and pickup zone.

## Phase 3: Copywriting (Title + Description)

### Title Formula (max 80 chars):
`[emoji] [KEY FEATURE] + [Model] + [Spec1] + [Spec2] + [Audience Keyword]`

Bad: "Vendo laptop ThinkPad" — 10,000 results.
Good: "🔥 Workstation ThinkPad P50 16GB i7 AutoCAD Revit Ingeniería"

Keywords: model name, key spec, program names, audience keyword.

### Description Structure (AIDA formula):

```
⚡ [POSITIONING STATEMENT] — NOT A COMMON [ITEM TYPE]

[1 line explaining WHAT this is and WHO it's for.]

📋 SPECIFICATIONS (scannable bullets)
• Model:
• Processor:
• RAM / Storage:
• GPU:
• OS: [LICENSE TYPE]
• Included software:

✅ WHAT'S INCLUDED
✔ [item 1]
✔ ✨ [BONUS - the hook]

🎁 WHY THIS OVER A NEW CHEAP ITEM?
• [build quality]
• [included software value]
• [expandability / durability]

💰 PRICE (bundle options)
• Base: $XXX
• With [software]: $XXX

📍 LOGISTICS
• Pickup: [safe location]
• Payment: [methods]
• NO: shipping, trades, holds

📩 Message me what you'll use it for — I'll confirm if it works.
```

## Phase 4: Pricing Strategy

### Price Anchoring
- List **10-15% above minimum acceptable**
- Show **included software value** so item itself looks cheap
- Perceived value math

### The Decoy Tactic
Offer 2-3 bundles. The middle option makes the full option look like a steal.

### Price Anchoring by Day
| Day | Action | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (Thu) | Max price | Quality buyers |
| 3 (Sat) | Renew + slight drop | Weekend traffic |
| 5 (Mon) | Relist NEW with 10-15% drop | Fresh algorithmic boost |
| 7 | Re-price at low end | Price is wrong |

## Phase 4B: Paid Facebook Promotion (Marketplace Boost & Ads Manager)

The skill up to this point covers **organic** selling strategy. When you have a small budget ($8-12 USD / S/30-45) and want to amplify a listing, use **paid promotion**. Facebook offers three ad paths — choose based on your goal.

### Path A: Marketplace Boost (Recommended for single-item selling)

The "Promocionar/Impulsar" button inside your Marketplace listing. Simplest path.

| Setting | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | "Get more messages" (not "Reach") |
| **Daily budget** | $1.50-2.40 USD |
| **Duration** | 5-7 days — longer gives the algorithm time to optimize |
| **Location radius** | 15-20 km in your city (more is wasted on single-item sales) |
| **Age/Gender** | Default (no manual restriction) |
| **Audience** | Automatic (Facebook targets based on listing category + behavior) |
| **Placement** | Marketplace search results + "Recommended" section only (NOT the newsfeed) |
| **Cost in Peru/LATAM** | CPM $0.30-1.50 per 1,000 impressions. $10 buys ~10,000-15,000 impressions |

**When to use this:** You have one item, a small budget, minimal setup time. The algorithm auto-targets people who recently browsed similar products.

**Limitations:** No newsfeed placement. Cannot target by interest ("estudiante de arquitectura"). The targeting is based on listing category + location + behavior, not demographics or interests.

**Optimization for $8-12 budget:**
- Run as a single 5-7 day campaign ($1.50-2.40/day) — NOT split into multiple short boosts.
- Refresh the listing (edit photos or title) before re-boosting if the first 48h yield zero messages — Facebook treats changes as a fresh signal.
- **Monitor at 48h**: 0 messages → pause, fix listing (price? photos?), then re-boost. Do not burn budget on a broken listing.

### Path B: Facebook Ads Manager (Advanced, requires Facebook Page)

Use the full Ads Manager (business.facebook.com/adsmanager) to create a campaign promoting your Marketplace listing. More setup, more control.

**What you gain vs. Boost:**
- Segment by **interests** — target people interested in "AutoCAD", "Revit", "ingeniería civil", "arquitectura", "construcción", "ThinkPad", "Lenovo"
- Segment by **job titles** — "ingeniero civil", "arquitecto", "diseñador 3D"
- **Multiple placements** — newsfeed, Instagram, Marketplace, Messenger
- **Retargeting** — show ads to people who messaged but didn't buy

**What you lose vs. Boost:**
- Setup time (30-60 min vs 2 min)
- Requires a Facebook Business Page connected to Ads Manager
- Steeper learning curve for objective selection and creative

**Minimum budget for Ads Manager (Peru):** $3-5/day recommended. $8-12 total covers only 2-3 days — tight for the learning phase. Better for sellers with $30+ budgets or multiple items.

**When to use this:** You have several items to sell (spread the setup cost), or a high-value item (>$500) where precise targeting recovers the ad spend.

### Path C: Boosted Posts in Groups

**Do not rely on this.** Since 2021-2022, you cannot target ads specifically at members of a Facebook Group. Boost from a group only works if:
- You are the group admin AND the group has monetization enabled
- The post was shared from a connected Page

**Better alternative:** Post FOR FREE in 3-5 relevant groups (engineering, architecture, buy/sell local) — this is already covered under cross-posting. Groups are for organic reach, not paid.

### Budget allocation decision (for single item, $8-12 total)

| Scenario | Best use of budget |
|---|---|
| First-time seller, single item | 100% Marketplace Boost (Path A) for 5-7 days |
| Item didn't sell after 7 days organic | Boost for 5 days + use $2-3 for in-person coffee/demo (Phase 6) |
| Multiple items ($30+ budget) | Ads Manager (Path B) with interest targeting |
| High urgency (need to sell in 48h) | Boost at max daily budget ($3-4/day) for 2-3 days |

**Rule of thumb:** If you're spending $8-12 on one item, 80% of the value comes from listing quality (photos, title, price). Paid promotion only amplifies what's already good — it cannot fix a bad listing.

## Phase 5: Communication Scripts

**Golden rule: respond in <15 minutes.**

### Opening — "Sigue disponible?"
❌ "Sí."
✅ "¡Hola! Sí. Dime, ¿para qué programa/proyecto la necesitas?"

Ask an OPEN question — reveals their persona.

### Lowball — "Te doy $XXX" (30%+ less)
✅ "Gracias. Considerando que [software] suma ~$XXX, lo más bajo que puedo hacer si vienes este fin de semana es $XXX."

### Objection — "¿Por qué la vendes?"
❌ "Necesito dinero."
✅ "Actualicé por [reason implying the item is good]. No por fallas."

### Objection — "Es muy vieja/año"
✅ Acknowledge age, reframe with analogy (work truck vs sedan), explain why specs beat year.

### Ghosting Recovery (24h later)
✅ "Bajé a $XXX hasta [day]. Si te interesa, avísame."

### Close With Scarcity
✅ "Otro interesado quiere verla [day]. Si confirmas antes de [time], te la reservo."

---

## Phase 6: Logistics + In-Person Close

### Scheduling — Offer 2 specific options.
### Pre-Close Prep — Item charged, USB test files, charger, payment app.
### The 3-Minute Script:
1. Greet + hand over item. Stay silent.
2. "¿Quieres que abra algo pesado?" → USB test.
3. "Si te la llevas hoy: $XXX." Close.
### Coffee Investment — $2-3 drink for reciprocity.
### Safety — Public place. No shipping. Verify payment in YOUR app.

---

## Phase 7: Post-Sale
Deactivate listing. Delete cross-posts. Block ghosters (optional). Ask for recommendation if positive.

---

## Pitfalls
- Describing specs without context for the buyer's use case.
- Too many messages with no close → price is too high, not a listing problem.
- Responding defensively to lowballs. State anchor and move on.
- Skipping the in-person demo. A buyer who tests is 90% likely to buy.
- Giving your home address instead of a public mall.

## Verification (48h metrics)
- 0-3 messages → bad photos/title. Rework.
- 10+ messages, 0 visits → price too high or description weak.
- 5+ visits, 0 sales → condition or presentation. Improve in-person.
- 20+ messages, visits, no sales → price fundamentally wrong. Drop 15-25%.
